## 2026-09-24 fresh intake

### QRNG security becomes an evidence/provenance problem
ETSI published TR 104 171 on 2026-09-24 with implementation guidance for Quantum Random Number Generators. It explicitly warns that statistically random-looking output can still be predictable to an adversary with side information and covers entropy-source modelling/validation, extraction, runtime monitoring, drift/bias/failure detection, tamper/side-channel concerns and provenance. It is an ETSI Technical Report/guidance document, not a new Internet-generation standard.

**Opportunity:** train Sentinel to reason about cryptographic primitive provenance and runtime health rather than trusting labels such as “quantum randomness”.
**Threat:** downstream systems can inherit compromised entropy while all higher-level cryptography appears formally correct.
**Competitor gap:** connect physical/source trust, extraction, attestation, runtime monitoring, key generation and resulting cryptographic effects in one causal graph.
**Benchmark idea:** `ENTROPY_PROVENANCE`: determine whether a key/signature/session is trustworthy given entropy-source evidence, side information, extraction and runtime-health signals.

### AINS makes discovery metadata richer—and therefore easier to over-trust
IETF Internet-Draft `draft-vandemeent-ains-discovery-02`, published 2026-09-24, proposes agent discovery records carrying identity, capability, endpoint, route-posture and cryptographic evidence references.

**Opportunity:** benchmark discovery-to-authority reasoning across richer metadata.
**Threat:** a resolved record can be authentic yet stale, over-privileged, maliciously provisioned or insufficient to authorize an effect.
**Competitor gap:** distinguish `resolution authenticity != identity proof != current delegation != action authorization != safe effect`.
**Benchmark idea:** `DISCOVERY_AUTHORITY_GAP`.

### Tezos Quantumnet exposes heterogeneous PQ migration risk
Tezos' experimental Quantumnet went live 2026-09-24, combining ML-DSA-44 tz5 accounts with PQ-oriented changes to consensus-related cryptography and DAL components.

**Opportunity:** real training case for whole-system crypto dependency/migration reasoning.
**Threat:** partial migrations create mixed classical/PQ assumptions and compatibility/fallback hazards.
**Benchmark idea:** `PQ_COMPONENT_GRAPH`: infer remaining classical dependencies and migration ordering from component-level evidence.

### QSB optimization shows benchmark success is not deployment success
Current Quantum-Safe Bitcoin challenge results put estimated construction cost near $66–67, but the optimized figure is benchmark-derived; QSB transactions remain nonstandard for normal relay and require direct miner submission.

**Opportunity:** teach Sentinel to separate cryptographic construction, benchmark, policy acceptance and actual deployment.
**Threat:** security claims can outrun operational validity.
**Benchmark idea:** `SECURITY_CLAIM_SCOPE`: identify exactly what a mitigation protects, under which operational assumptions, and what remains exposed.

## Standards reality
No accepted IETF/W3C Web4/Web5/Web6/Web7/Web8 generation standard was identified in the last 24 hours. AINS is an Informational Internet-Draft. ETSI TR 104 171 is technical guidance. Quantumnet and QSB are experimental/deployment research artifacts, not new Internet-generation standards.
