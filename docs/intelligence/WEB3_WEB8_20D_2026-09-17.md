# Web3–Web8 20-Day Intelligence — Koschei Sentinel

Window: 2026-08-29 → 2026-09-20

Sentinel is treated here as a cybersecurity foundation model, not a scanner.

## 2026-09-20 fresh intake

### HJS/JEP/AAP agent-accountability drafts hit the lifecycle boundary
IETF Datatracker shows the HJS accountability draft (`draft-wang-hjs-accountability-01`) expiring 2026-09-20. Its model chains Judge, Delegate, Terminate and Verify events into cryptographic responsibility receipts. The related JEP revision expired 2026-09-19, while AAP adds task-to-task causality through `task_based_on` and is also at its September lifecycle boundary. These are Internet-Drafts/individual proposal work, not RFCs or adopted standards.

**Opportunity:** add `ACCOUNTABILITY_CAUSAL_CHAIN` benchmark cases that separate who authenticated, who delegated, which judgment/task caused the next task, who terminated authority, and which evidence proves responsibility after an effect.

**Threat:** signed receipts can still encode an incomplete or stale causal story; cryptographic integrity of an accountability record is not proof that authority, state, or effect was valid.

**Competitor gap:** combine accountability/provenance reasoning with Sentinel's existing finality-bound authority, artifact identity, external-state provenance and incident reconstruction instead of treating receipts as a standalone audit log.

**Product/model idea:** train graph problems over `principal -> judge/agent -> delegation -> task/judgment -> downstream task -> termination/revocation -> effect -> receipt`, with adversarially incomplete evidence and status/lifecycle metadata.

### Plugin4Shell status remains an artifact-identity problem, not a new protocol
A 2026-09-20 follow-up reports that the previously disclosed Plugin4Shell issue affected multiple coding-agent plugin flows and that some vendors have patched while others were still reported exposed. This is a follow-up to the already-ingested 17–18 Sep disclosure, so it is not counted as a new finding. Retain the existing `ARTIFACT_IDENTITY_CONTINUITY` benchmark and track vendor patch status separately.

## Standards reality
No accepted IETF/W3C generation standard named Web4, Web5, Web6, Web7 or Web8 was identified in this intake. HJS/JEP/AAP are Internet-Draft proposal families, not Internet Standards. Draft expiry/lifecycle is evidence that protocol ideas must be learned independently from transient document names.
